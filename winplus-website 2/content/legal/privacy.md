WinPlus Education & Technology Inc. ("WinPlus", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and our services, how we use it and the choices you have.

## Information we collect

- **Information you give us**, such as your name, email address, phone or WeChat ID, the student's grade and interests, and any message you send through our consultation, event registration or newsletter forms.
- **Information about students**, such as academic records, activities and application materials, when families engage our services.
- **Technical information**, such as your browser type, device and pages visited, collected automatically by our hosting provider for security and performance.

## How we use information

- To respond to inquiries and schedule consultations
- To provide counseling, programs and events you sign up for
- To send newsletters and event invitations if you subscribe (you can unsubscribe at any time)
- To improve our website and services
- To meet legal obligations
