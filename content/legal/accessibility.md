WinPlus Education and Technology Inc. (“WinPlus”) is committed to making the website at [insert website URL] (the “Website”) accessible to all users, including people with disabilities.

## 1. Standard

WinPlus aims for the Website to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. The Website is available in English and Chinese, and each language version is subject to this commitment.

## 2. Measures

WinPlus supports accessibility by:


- incorporating accessibility requirements into Website design and development;
- providing text alternatives for images and captions or transcripts for pre-recorded video, where feasible;
- supporting keyboard navigation and compatibility with common screen readers; and
- providing white papers in accessible formats or, on request, in an alternative format.

## 3. Known Limitations

[insert any known limitations identified in testing, or delete this Section.] Some third-party content and services, including the payment processor’s checkout page and embedded media, are outside WinPlus’ control and may not fully conform to WCAG 2.1 Level AA.

## 4. Feedback and Assistance

If you experience difficulty accessing any part of the Website, or would like information in an alternative format, please contact WinPlus at [insert contact email]. Please describe the page and the issue you encountered. WinPlus will make reasonable efforts to respond promptly and to provide the information or service through an accessible alternative.
