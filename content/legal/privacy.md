WinPlus Education and Technology Inc. (“WinPlus,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects the privacy of the students, parents, guardians, schools, and other visitors who use our website at [insert website URL] (the “Website”) and our educational programs and services (together with the Website, the “Services”). This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use and share it, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights available to you. Disclosures for specific countries and regions appear in Section 12.

## 1. Who We Are

WinPlus is a New York-based education company. WinPlus is responsible for the personal information processed under this Privacy Policy and acts as its “controller” or “personal information handler” under applicable law. WinPlus has appointed a Privacy Officer who is responsible for overseeing compliance with this Privacy Policy and serves as WinPlus’ data protection officer for all jurisdictions in which WinPlus operates. Contact details appear in Section 14.

This Privacy Policy does not apply to third-party websites, platforms, or services linked from the Website, which are governed by their own privacy policies.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide


- **Contact and inquiry information:**  name, email address, telephone number, messaging account (e.g., WeChat ID), country or region, preferred language, and the content of your message.
- **Student information:**  student name, date of birth, grade level, school, academic interests, and parent or guardian contact details, provided by a parent or guardian.
- **Purchase information:**  billing name, billing address, and transaction details. Payments are processed by our third-party payment processor.
- **Newsletter and white paper requests:**  name, email address, organization (if applicable), and communication preferences.
- **School and partner information:**  name, title, institution, and business contact details of representatives of schools and organizations that contact us about collaboration.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit the Website, our hosting provider and analytics tools automatically collect technical information, including IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring URL, pages viewed, and the date and time of access. See Section 6 and our Cookie Policy.

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive personal information from schools and partners that refer students to us, from our payment processor (e.g., confirmation of payment), and from parents or guardians about their children.

### 2.4 Sensitive Information

We do not request sensitive personal information through the Website, such as government identification numbers, health information, or financial account numbers, and ask that you not submit it. Personal information of children under 14 is treated as sensitive personal information under certain laws, including the PRC Personal Information Protection Law, and is processed only as described in Section 7.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:


- respond to inquiries and provide information about our Services;
- process enrollments, purchases, payments, and refunds;
- deliver, administer, and improve our programs and Services;
- send newsletters, white papers, and marketing communications where you have consented or as otherwise permitted by law;
- communicate with schools and partners about collaboration;
- operate, secure, and analyze the performance of the Website;
- comply with legal, tax, accounting, and child-safeguarding obligations; and
- establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

## 4. Legal Bases for Processing

Where applicable law requires a legal basis for processing (including the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation and the PRC Personal Information Protection Law), we rely on the following:


- **Contract:**  to provide Services that you or your child have enrolled in and to process payments.
- **Consent:**  for marketing communications, non-essential cookies, processing of children’s personal information where consent is required, and any processing for which applicable law requires separate consent. You may withdraw consent at any time; withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal.
- **Legitimate interests:**  to respond to inquiries, communicate with schools and partners, and secure and improve the Website, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
- **Legal obligation:**  to comply with tax, accounting, safeguarding, and other legal requirements.

## 5. How We Share Information

We share personal information only as follows:


- **Service providers:**  website hosting (Netlify, Inc.), payment processing ([insert payment processor]), email and newsletter delivery ([insert email provider]), website analytics ([insert analytics provider]), and the video conferencing and learning platforms used to deliver our programs. Service providers may use personal information only to provide services to WinPlus and are bound by confidentiality and data-protection obligations.
- **Program partners:**  schools, host institutions, and program partners, solely as necessary to deliver a program in which a student is enrolled.
- **Legal and safety:**  where required by law, court order, or government authority, or where necessary to protect the safety of a student or another person, including mandatory reporting obligations.
- **Business transfers:**  in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to equivalent privacy protections and notice where required by law.
- **With consent:**  in any other circumstance with your consent.

## 6. Cookies, Analytics, and Embedded Content

The Website uses strictly necessary cookies required for security and checkout functions and, with your consent, analytics tools that help us understand how visitors use the Website. Non-essential cookies are disabled by default.

Embedded content from third parties (such as videos or payment forms) may set cookies or collect information under those parties’ own privacy policies. For details, see our Cookie Policy at [insert link].

Because no common industry standard exists for “Do Not Track” browser signals, the Website does not respond to them.

## 7. Children’s Privacy

### 7.1 Children Under 13 or 14

The Website is directed to parents, guardians, schools, and students. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from a child under 13, or under 14 where applicable law sets that age (including in the PRC and Quebec), or under the age of digital consent in the child’s country of residence, without verifiable consent from the child’s parent or legal guardian.

### 7.2 Children’s Personal Information Rules

WinPlus applies the following rules to personal information of children: (a) we collect only the personal information reasonably necessary for the child to participate in the relevant program; (b) access is limited to designated personnel with a need to know; (c) we do not disclose a child’s personal information to third parties for purposes not integral to the Services without separate parental consent; (d) we do not use a child’s personal information for advertising; and (e) we retain it only as described in Section 9.

### 7.3 Parental Rights

A parent or guardian may review the personal information we hold about their child, request its correction or deletion, refuse further collection or use, and withdraw consent by contacting us as described in Section 14.

## 8. Photos, Video, Testimonials, and Case Studies

We may photograph or record programs, including online sessions, in accordance with our Online Class Recording and Content Usage Policy and the consents provided at enrollment. We may also photograph or record, and publish accounts of, events and activities that WinPlus organizes or co-hosts, or in which WinPlus students or personnel participate, including conferences and events hosted by third parties. We use a student’s name, image, testimonial, identifiable case study, or individual admissions results in marketing materials only with the prior written consent of the student (if 18 or older) or the student’s parent or guardian. Case studies from which names, images, and other details that could reasonably identify a student have been removed may be published without further consent. To ask us to stop using an image or testimonial, contact us as described in Section 14. We will honor reasonable requests for future use; materials already published or distributed may not be fully retrievable.

## 9. How Long We Keep Information

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy or as required by law:

Personal information of children is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant program and meet legal obligations, and is never retained indefinitely. At the end of the applicable period, personal information is securely deleted or de-identified in accordance with our Document Retention and Destruction Policy.

## 10. International Data Transfers

WinPlus is based in the United States, and the Website is hosted in the United States. Personal information you submit is collected directly by WinPlus and processed in the United States and in other countries where our service providers operate, whose data protection laws may differ from those of your jurisdiction. We protect transferred personal information as described in this Privacy Policy and use contractual safeguards with service providers where required. Where applicable law requires your separate consent to the processing of personal information outside your jurisdiction (including the PRC Personal Information Protection Law), we will request it at the point of collection.

## 11. Your Choices and Rights

### 11.1 Marketing Communications

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in each email or by contacting us. You will continue to receive transactional and program-related communications.

### 11.2 Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to: (a) know what personal information we hold about you and obtain a copy; (b) correct inaccurate information; (c) delete your information; (d) restrict or object to certain processing; (e) receive your information in a portable format; (f) withdraw consent; and (g) receive an explanation of our processing rules. These rights are subject to legal exceptions and record-keeping obligations.

### 11.3 Submitting a Request

Submit requests to info@winplus.org. We will verify your identity before acting on a request and will respond within the period required by applicable law. You may also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your jurisdiction.
